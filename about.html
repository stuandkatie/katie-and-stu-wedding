Our Story

We met on Bumble in early 2023. The connection was instant, two adventure lovers swapping stories of travels near and far. After a lot of dates filled with laughter and good conversation, we decided to put the relationship to the test with a road trip to Asheville for Katie's 32nd birthday, just a few months after we met. We laughed the whole way and never argued once. We both thought, okay, we might actually have a shot at this.

So naturally we decided to stress-test it again, this time internationally. Just after our one-year anniversary we flew to Norway to chase the Northern Lights. Another success. Two big trips, zero meltdowns, and Stu started to think Katie might be the one.

One more stress test was in order: a road trip to Sedona. Stu had secretly booked a private engagement tour at a vineyard, and under a beautiful pergola he dropped to one knee and got as far as "Will you mar" before Katie shouted "YES!"

Since we met we've traveled through 14 states and 10 countries together, two of which Stu drove across himself. And we're just getting started.

We'd love for you to join us as we celebrate our next big adventure with the people we love.
